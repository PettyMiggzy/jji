How to read it

Businesses = listings of your chosen type in that ZIP (open Overture Maps data).

Competitors = junk removal, dumpster rental and junkyard listings in the same ZIP.

Whitespace = businesses divided by (competitors + 1). Higher means more potential customers per competitor.

Use it to choose where to advertise or hire first, then run a territory sweep on the top ZIPs. Listings can be months old, so treat counts as a guide, not a census.
